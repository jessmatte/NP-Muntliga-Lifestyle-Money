Useful Phrases & Starters

Practice Aid Only — Not available on the real National Test (Delprov A). Use these sentence starters to build confidence during practice!

1. Summarizing Your Card (Part 1A)

  • • "My card is about a person named..."
  • • "The main point on my card is that..."
  • • "He/She thinks it is better to... because..."
  • • "According to my card, many young people..."

2. Giving Your Opinion & Examples

  • • "I completely agree with this because..."
  • • "I partly agree, but on the other hand..."
  • • "I see it differently because..."
  • • "From my own experience, I know that..."
  • • "For example, a friend of mine..."

3. Keeping the Conversation Going (Interaktion)

  • • "What do you think about that?"
  • • "Do you agree with what my card says?"
  • • "Have you ever experienced something similar?"
  • • "Why do you think people make that choice?"
  • • "That's a good point, and I also think..."

4. Part 2 Discussion Starters

  • • "I'd like to talk about [topic] first."
  • • "For me personally, this is important because..."
  • • "The biggest advantage / disadvantage is..."
  • • "If everyone in society did this, I think..."
  • • "Shall we pick another topic? Which one do you want?"
